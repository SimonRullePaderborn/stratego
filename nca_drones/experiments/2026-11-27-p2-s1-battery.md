# Experiment card: 2026-11-27-p2-s1-battery

## Before the run

* **Phase and gate it serves:** phase 2, step 1 of the chain in `PROGRAMME.md` section 12.4.
* **Hypothesis (one sentence):** showing the lattice the seeker's battery level does not lower closed-loop success
  on the 2,000 never-posed tasks, compared with the measured recipe of step 0.
* **What would support it:** the closed-loop rate of this run (bf16, its own 2,000 never-posed tasks) is not below
  the mean rate of the three runs of step 0 by more than max(2 s, 2 points), where s is the standard deviation of
  those three rates (rule of section 12.4; s is known when step 0 ends).
* **What would refute it:** a rate further below. Then the run is looked at on trajectories before step 2 is built
  on it.
* **The one thing that changes** relative to step 0, seed 0: `--battery 1`. The lattice gets a fourth input map: on
  the seeker's cell, its battery level divided by the capacity of 5. The level is the simulator's own (`env.bat`):
  full in slice 0, as every episode starts, and the reported level in every slice of the realised past, which
  exists in closed-loop replanning and in the 60% of training samples that start from a realised past. The first
  convolution gains 1,728 weights (38,130 to 39,858 in all). Task, rule, search, credit, shared noise, evaluation
  sets and seed are those of step 0.
* **Also reported, with no threshold:** the size of any gain, which is what the battery input is worth on this task
  (a reviewer's point: it may make the task easier and so hide a loss elsewhere); closed loop against open loop,
  since the new map carries information only once an episode is running; the paired difference to step 0, seed 0,
  on the 200-task test set, which is the same for both.
* **Verification done on the cluster's CPUs first:** `test_sim.py`, which now ends with `test_battery_input`: a
  hand-worked episode (levels 5, 4, 3, 5, 4, 3, 2 with the refill at the meeting), the map's values for a past of
  four steps, and the battery trace of `rollout` against a replay in the simulator.
* **How it runs:** one Docker container, CPU only, 16 cores, from `~/wf/runs/p2s1` filled from the Mac:
  `bash cluster/jobs/p2_old_recipe.sh p2s1_battery_s0 0 0.5 --battery 1`. It may run while step 0 is still running;
  it is judged after step 0.
* **Commit:** the commit that holds this card, named in `~/wf/runs/p2s1/COMMIT`.
* **Cluster job ids:** container `wf-p2s1-s0`.   **Estimated GPU-hours:** 0 (CPU only, about 2.5 hours)

## After the run

Run on 27 November 2026 from commit `65d04ba` in `~/wf/runs/p2s1` (97 of 97 files), container `wf-p2s1-s0`, CPU
only. Files: `results/p2/p2s1_battery_s0.*`.

* **Result:** closed loop on its 2,000 never-posed tasks: 1,650 = 82.5% (bf16; Wilson 95% interval 80.8 to 84.1) and
  1,654 (fp32). Open loop: 1,512 = 75.6%. Test set: 174 of 200 closed, 162 open. Chosen checkpoint: round 850. The
  three runs of step 0 gave 75.35%, 70.45% and 70.05%: mean 71.95%, standard deviation s = 2.95 points, so the
  rule's margin is max(2 s, 2) = 5.9 points. Validation success (100 tasks, closed loop) after 100, 300, 600 and 900
  rounds: 0.24, 0.62, 0.69, 0.89. About 6.1 s per round on two threads.
* **Trajectories looked at:** none; the step is not in question.
* **Supported, refuted or inconclusive:** supported. The run is 10.6 points above the baseline's mean and 7.2 above
  its best seed, which has the same seed 0.
* **What was learned:** showing the battery level is worth about 7 to 10 points on this task, more than three times
  the run-to-run spread. A reviewer's warning is confirmed: every later number has to be compared with this step,
  not with the stored 75.4%, which was moreover the best of three seeds. This is one seed; the card
  `2026-11-27-p2-codes-by-width` adds three GPU seeds of the same recipe.
