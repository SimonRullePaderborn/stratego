# Experiment card: 2026-11-28-p2-s8-kept-ks8

## Before the run

* **Phase and gate it serves:** phase 2, step 8 of the chain in `PROGRAMME.md` section 12.4, run again in full with
  the dial that the card `2026-11-27-p2-s8-kept-dials` selected, and the iteration gate of section 14.1.
* **What was seen:** step 8 as first run (4 iterations per step) scored 19.9% and is not kept. In the short runs of
  the dials card the same recipe with 8 iterations per step solved 83% of the easy tasks with the rule alone over
  rounds 125 to 149, where 4 per step solved 15% and regrowth about 90%; back-propagation through the whole replay
  at 4 per step solved 17%.
* **Hypothesis (one sentence):** keeping the lattice's state from step to step with 8 iterations after each new
  observation, in place of regrowing with 32, does not lower closed-loop success on the 2,000 never-posed tasks,
  compared with step 7.
* **What would support it:** the rule of section 12.4, applied to means of three seeds: the mean closed-loop rate
  of the three runs of this card (bf16, each on its own 2,000 never-posed tasks) is not below the mean of step 7's
  three seeds by more than max(2 e, 2 points), with e the standard error of the difference of the two means,
  computed from the pooled standard deviation of the six runs.
* **What would refute it:** a mean further below. Then step 8 is reported as failed with its dials tried, nothing is
  added to the learner, and the PI decides how the chain goes on.
* **The one thing that changes** relative to step 7: `--kept 1 --ks 8` (with `--ks_jitter 2 --grad_its 32`, the
  defaults): a volume is grown once with 32 iterations on the first observation; after every step the new cells
  and battery level are burned in and the rule runs 8 iterations on the state it has (6 to 10 in learning, redrawn
  for every batch), with gradient through the last 32 iterations of the replay. Everything else is step 7's.
  Step 7 has one seed so far, so its recipe is run with seeds 1 and 2 as well; those two runs change nothing.
* **Also reported, with no threshold in this card:** the iteration gate for all five new checkpoints (every
  iteration count times 2, 4 and 8, paired on the same tasks). For the kept runs it is the gate of phase 2:
  passed if the lower 95% bound of the paired difference is above -2 points at all three multiples. For step 7's
  seeds it shows whether the collapse of seed 0 at four times the iterations belongs to the recipe. Further: the
  seconds per round against step 7, and the standard deviation of each recipe's three rates, which becomes the
  yardstick for the steps that follow.
* **What the result decides:** whether step 9, the sliding window, is built on a state kept with 8 iterations per
  step.
* **Verification done on the cluster's CPUs first:** `test_sim.py` in the run folder. The runs check themselves:
  the first 151 rounds of `p2s8_ks8_s0` must equal the log of `p2k_ks8_s0` from the dials card in every field
  (the same seed, flags and training code).
* **How it runs:** five Docker containers, one per idle GPU, each after the check on the host that nothing else
  holds the GPU and with the guard inside, from `~/wf/runs/p2s8k` filled from the Mac. With F for
  `--battery 1 --codes 1 --C 32 --hid 128 --threads 8 --sim wf --device cuda --closed_search 1 --update bound`:
  `bash cluster/jobs/p2_old_recipe.sh p2s8_ks8_s<seed> <seed> 0.5 F --kept 1 --ks 8` for seeds 0, 1 and 2, and
  `bash cluster/jobs/p2_old_recipe.sh p2s7_bound_s<seed> <seed> 0.5 F` for seeds 1 and 2; each followed in its
  container by `bash cluster/jobs/p2_iteration_gate.sh <run>`.
* **Commit:** the commit that holds this card, named in `~/wf/runs/p2s8k/COMMIT`.
* **Cluster job ids:** containers `wf-p2s8k-s0`, `-s1`, `-s2`, `wf-p2s7-s1`, `-s2`.
  **Estimated GPU-hours:** about 8 (three runs of 1.2 hours, two of 2 hours, and the gates)
